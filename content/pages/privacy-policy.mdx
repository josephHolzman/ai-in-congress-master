AI in Congress is a personal, independent publication. It doesn't ask you for any information, and it doesn't track you.

## What this site collects

Nothing directly. There are no accounts, no comment forms, no newsletter sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no cookies.

## Hosting

The site is hosted by [Vercel](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy). Like any web host, Vercel's servers process basic request information, such as your IP address, browser type, and the page you asked for, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. I don't use that information to identify or track visitors.

## Fonts and other resources

Fonts and images are served from this site itself, so loading a page doesn't send your information to a font or image service.

## Links to other sites

Articles link to sources such as Congress.gov, court opinions, and news outlets. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply when you visit them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, for example because I add a newsletter, the updated policy will be posted here with a new date.
